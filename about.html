About Riviera Laser Studios

Meet Dr. Jeannine Jenkins, MD

A national trainer and 20-year veteran of facial injectables and laser medicine, based in Dana Point, California.

Founder & Medical Director

A Master of the Art of Facial Rejuvenation

Dr. Jeannine Jenkins is the founder of Riviera Laser Studios and a leading expert in natural, full-face rejuvenation using long-lasting facial fillers and advanced laser resurfacing techniques. With more than 20 years of experience with facial injectables and lasers, she is a national trainer and a true master of the art of facial rejuvenation.

Riviera Laser Studios specializes in safe, minimally-invasive techniques for personalized results — without the look, cost, or downtime of a traditional surgical facelift.

Every consultation begins with an in-depth discussion of the facial aging process, your individual concerns, and your goals — so Dr. Jenkins can build a customized treatment plan personalized to you.

Our Method

The Global Facial Rejuvenation Approach

To achieve a natural, youthful look without an overdone appearance, Dr. Jenkins takes a conservative, global approach to volumize and restore what time takes away.

01

Rebuild the Foundation

Start by rebuilding volume loss deep below the skin to reconstruct the facial foundation.

02

Contour the Center

Contour soft tissue areas like the cheeks and tear troughs to restore the central face.

03

Refine the Details

Finish by refining lines and wrinkles for an overall younger look, with lasting, natural results.
